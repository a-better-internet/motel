Porting checklist

To start a new world from this engine:

  1. Copy the shell and the engine files: index.html (markup, CSS, HUD), 00–06, 11, and 15–19. Keep three.js r128, or test carefully before upgrading; the tone-mapping patch replaces a shader chunk by its exact text.
  2. Delete the content files (07–14j except 11) and remove their script tags.
  3. Cut or stub the content hooks in the core files. These names are defined by motel content but read by the engine:
    • 06-collision: POOL, PAVED and poolFloorY in groundY; TRAFFIC for the traffic colliders.
    • 16-lighting: POOL_LIGHTS.
    • 17-player: POOL (wading) and OFFICE (the desk bell).
    • 18-hud: ROOMS (vacancy), OFFICE, POOL.
    • 19-loop: the pool's waterMat, poolFloorCaustic and poolGlow; NEON, VAC_NEON, TVWIN_MAT, SLOW_GREEN; BASE in the debug handle.
    • 15-bake: the rooftop clutter after the bake reads lvlY, WALL_H1, NORTH and EAST.
    • The cars' constants and builder (CARC, CAR_RIDE, CARS, driving) live in 10-office.js; move them to a file of their own before 11-driving.js.
  4. Set the config: SPAWN, EYE, PLAYER_R, your site constants, and NEXT_SCENE_URL if the world hands off to another page.
  5. Repaint the cycle: DayNight.P, the night haze, the start time and DUR.
  6. Replace the land: a new heightAt (base noise, features, pads), ring boxes on one lattice, and SKIRT_GAPS for anything you dig.
  7. Draw your textures in 02-textures.js, using the three-pass recipe, and declare their buckets in 15-bake.js.
  8. Build content files in order, one place per file, each in its own local frame, registering colliders, flats, zones, lamps, doors and seats as you go.
  9. Keep the light budget: one key, twelve pool lamps, and glow for everything else.
  10. Keep the debug handle (window.MOTEL or your own) and run the four checks on everything you add.

Twelve sentences to remember

  1. Paint the colour; let the light shade it; never let it clip.
  2. An emissive does not illuminate.
  3. A lamp with a volume is on only while you are inside it.
  4. Do not raise the normal bias to cure acne; find what is shadowing itself.
  5. Under the ground there is no sky.
  6. You stand on the triangles that are drawn.
  7. Every slab needs a flat, and flats overlap.
  8. A hole goes through every course, including the ones you cannot see.
  9. A lintel stops where it is.
  10. A seat looks the other way from its chair.
  11. Two faces on one plane fight; two faces that butt do not.
  12. When a probe and a picture disagree, the probe is testing the wrong thing.
